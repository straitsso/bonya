There is a moment, a few minutes after you sit down, when the room settles. The heat stops feeling like weather and starts feeling like a place.

That is when the first ladle goes on.

## Waiting for the stones

A heater needs time. The stones at the centre of the basket must be properly hot, not just the ones on top, or the water simply hisses and disappears. We suggest forty minutes from cold, longer in winter.

## Less than you think

Half a ladle is usually enough. Pour slowly, towards the back of the stones, and let the steam find its way to you. If it stings, it was too much. If you barely notice it, try a little more next time.

The aim is not intensity. It is a soft, even warmth that arrives on your shoulders and stays.
