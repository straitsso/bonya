Most of a good sauna happens outside the sauna.

After ten or fifteen minutes in the heat, step out. A lake is ideal, a cold shower is fine, and in winter a few minutes standing barefoot on the grass does more than you would expect.

## Rest properly

Sit down. Wrap up if it is cold. Drink some water. Let your breathing slow and your heart rate settle before you go back in.

## Three rounds is plenty

Heat, cool, rest. Repeat twice more. Then stop, even if you feel you could carry on. The evening after a sauna is part of it too.
