Timber left outside will change. We would rather plan for that than pretend otherwise.

## Heat-treated, not chemically treated

Thermally modified ash is baked at around 200°C in a low-oxygen kiln. The process changes the structure of the wood so it takes on less water, moves less and resists rot, without any preservatives.

## Letting it silver

Left unfinished, the exterior turns a soft silver grey within a year or two. Many people prefer it. If you would rather keep the warmer tone, a light oil once a year is all it needs.

Inside, the aspen stays pale and cool to the touch, which is exactly what you want from a bench at 80°C.
