# Delivery & installation

Every Bonya sauna is delivered and installed by our own team. There is nothing to assemble yourself.

## Where we deliver

We install across mainland England, Wales and Scotland. For Northern Ireland, the islands and Europe, please [get in touch](/enquire) and we will quote individually.

## Before we arrive

Your sauna needs a firm, level base. A concrete pad, paving slabs or ground screws all work. Once your order is confirmed we send a simple drawing with the exact footprint, and can arrange groundwork through a local partner if you would prefer.

Electric models need a dedicated supply from your consumer unit, fitted by a qualified electrician. We are happy to talk your electrician through the requirements.

## Access

The sauna arrives in finished sections. The widest section needs a clear path of about 90 cm from the vehicle to the base. If access is tight, tell us early and we will plan around it.

## On the day

Installation usually takes one day. We assemble the sauna, fit the heater, test it with you and leave the site tidy.

## Lead times

Current lead times are shown on each model's page and confirmed when you order. We will keep you updated throughout the build.
