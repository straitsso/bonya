# Warranty

We build our saunas to last decades, and we stand behind them.

## What is covered

- **Structure and timber:** 10 years against structural defects, rot and failure of joinery.
- **Heater and electrics:** 2 years, in line with the manufacturer's warranty.
- **Glass, doors and fittings:** 2 years against defects in materials or workmanship.

## What is not covered

Natural changes to timber are not defects. Exterior wood will silver, move slightly with the seasons and develop small surface checks over time. The warranty also does not cover damage from misuse, unsuitable foundations or alterations made by others.

## Looking after your sauna

Keep the vents clear, let the sauna dry out with the door open after use, and wipe the benches occasionally. A light exterior oil once a year will keep the warmer timber tone if you prefer it to silvering.

## Making a claim

Email us with your order number, a short description and a few photos. We will reply within two working days and, where needed, arrange a visit.
